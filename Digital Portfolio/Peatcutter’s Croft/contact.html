How to Find Us

The Peatcutter's Croft is located in the small, beautiful township of Badrallach on the Dundonnell estate. It is about an hour from Inverness Airport with direct air links to major UK cities.

By Air

Flights to Inverness Airport are available from Heathrow, Gatwick, Luton, Edinburgh, Glasgow, and more. Collection can be arranged at a small cost.

By Rail

Nearest station: Garve (on the Inverness to Kyle route). 30 miles from us – pickup available.

By Road

From Inverness: A9 → A835 → A832 → single-track road to Badrallach. Approx. 1 hour journey.

By Ferry

Summer ferries: Ullapool–Altnaharrie (3 miles away) and Scoraig–Badluarach (on request). Other options via Braemore Junction or Westerbus.
