Street Outreach

Our volunteers operate weekly food runs in Leeds and Wakefield. We offer hot meals, snacks, and drinks to individuals living on the streets or in temporary accommodation.

Emergency Supplies

We provide seasonal packs including hats, gloves, blankets, sleeping bags, and toiletries. These are distributed especially during the winter months to ensure warmth and hygiene.

Wellbeing and Signposting

Our team takes time to talk, listen, and direct people toward local support services, including housing advice, mental health resources, and foodbanks.

Partnership Work

We collaborate with other organisations, faith groups and grassroots networks to share resources and respond collectively to the needs of vulnerable communities.
