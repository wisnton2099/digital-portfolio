Welcome

We are a charitable organisation based in West Yorkshire, offering practical support to homeless individuals and those in need. Our mission is to provide warm meals, essential supplies, and meaningful connection through outreach work.

Every week, our volunteers go out onto the streets to meet people where they are – distributing food, clothes, toiletries, and offering conversation and dignity. We believe that small acts of kindness can create lasting change.

How We Help

  • Hot food distribution in Leeds and Wakefield city centres
  • Provision of hygiene kits, blankets, socks, and gloves
  • Outreach support and signposting to local services
  • Seasonal support (e.g. winter packs, festive meals)
