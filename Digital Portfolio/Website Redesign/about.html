Who We Are

The InTouch Foundation is a grassroots organisation driven by a team of passionate volunteers who believe in making direct, meaningful impact in the lives of those who are homeless or vulnerable.

Our Vision

We envision a world where everyone, regardless of their circumstances, is treated with dignity and respect. Our foundation aims to bridge the gap between those in need and the services that can help them build a better future.

Our Story

Founded in West Yorkshire, our organisation has grown from a small group distributing sandwiches and socks, to a city-wide outreach team providing regular food runs, support, and advocacy. We continue to operate independently, relying on the kindness of donors and the dedication of volunteers.
